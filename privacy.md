# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

## Overview

Marathon Recovery Tracker is a personal, noncommercial application used to analyze Oura sleep, recovery, and activity information alongside Garmin running data.

## Data Collected

With the account holder's authorization, the application may retrieve sleep duration, sleep scores, readiness scores, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, and related Oura measurements.

## Data Storage and Use

The application is designed to store retrieved data locally on the account holder's personal computer. Data is used for personal fitness and marathon training analysis.

The application does not sell personal information, display health records publicly, or operate a public user database.

## Third-Party Sharing

The account holder may choose to upload selected data to third-party analysis services, including ChatGPT. Such sharing is initiated by the account holder and is subject to the selected service's privacy practices.

## Data Deletion

The account holder can delete locally stored records and revoke Oura authorization through the applicable account controls.

## Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator using the email address registered with the Oura developer application
