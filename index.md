# Marathon Recovery Tracker

Marathon Recovery Tracker is a personal, noncommercial project designed to analyze sleep, recovery, and fitness data from Oura alongside Garmin running activities.

The application is intended solely for its developer's personal use and is not available as a public service.

## Purpose

To explore relationships between sleep quality, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, readiness, and running performance during marathon training.
